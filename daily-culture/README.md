# Daily Culture

A home screen for daily puzzle games (NYT, Metazooa, Globle, Krillion and more) with per-game reset times. Every game you finish grows a colony on your petri dish.

🌐 **[Open in browser](https://avelaxonov.github.io/script-vault/daily-culture/)** · ⤓ **[Download for offline use](daily-culture.html)** (right-click → Save link as…, then double-click the file)

Everything is saved locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari or Chrome → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome → ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.
- **Windows / Mac (Chrome):** ⋮ → Cast, save, and share → **Install page as app**.

## Using it

- **Tap a game** to open it. It counts as done and grows a colony.
- **Green check** on a tile undoes it.
- **Red dot** means a new puzzle is waiting. Each game resets at its own time, for example Spelling Bee at 3 AM ET and the Mini at 10 PM ET.
- **⋯ menu:** edit games, share or move, bring back defaults.
  - In edit mode, tap a game to change its link or reset time, make it **optional**, **skip it today**, or move it to storage.
- **Bottom tray:**
  - **Plates:** calendar and gel view of past days, plus unlockable agar media.
  - **Calibrate:** weekly P20 check.
  - **70% EtOH:** wipes the bench every morning.

## Moving progress between devices

Progress is stored per device and per browser. To continue on another device: ⋯ → **Share or move** → **Copy progress**, then paste the code on the other device under "Got a code?".

To give friends your game list without your progress, use **Copy list**.

Note: the online version and a downloaded copy keep separate progress.
